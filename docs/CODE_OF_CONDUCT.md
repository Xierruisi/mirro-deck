# Code of Conduct

**English** | [简体中文](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.zh-CN.md)

MirroDeck is committed to providing a welcoming, professional, and safe collaborative environment for everyone, regardless of experience, identity, background, or viewpoint.

## Expected behavior

- Treat others with respect and keep discussions focused on facts and technical issues.
- Accept constructive feedback and explain disagreements clearly.
- Consider the interests of the community and its users.
- Proactively protect private information in logs, screenshots, and reproduction materials.
- Clarify misunderstandings, apologize for harm, and improve when problems arise.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, threats, discrimination, or personal attacks
- Publishing another person's private information without permission
- Sexualized content or unwelcome attention
- Spam, malicious disruption, intentional deception, or persistent interference with discussions
- Other conduct that is clearly inappropriate in a professional collaborative environment

## Scope and enforcement

This Code applies in the repository, Issues, Pull Requests, Discussions, and other public or private interactions where someone represents the project.

Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate content and may issue a reminder, warning, temporary restriction, or permanent ban depending on the severity. Enforcement decisions will consider the available facts, privacy, and the safety of those affected.

Use GitHub's content reporting and blocking tools to report conduct violations. If a report contains a security vulnerability, private data, or other information that should not be public, submit it privately through the repository's GitHub Security Advisory instead of opening a public Issue. Maintainers will handle reports carefully and share information only as needed to investigate and respond.
